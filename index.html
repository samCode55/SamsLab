Dead Men's Tales — Book I: Maneater

Or just Maneater for short.

Maneater is my current Work in Progress. It follows the stories of three main characters: Christopher, Nicole and Clyde. It takes place in a dystopian early- twentieth century city called Grand Guinevere. When Nicole, Christopher's sister, is kidnapped by a gang who runs a huge part of the city, he is determined to do anything it takes to get her back, no matter the cost.

When I first started writing Maneater, I was a completely different person. But throughout the months, all the sweat and brainstorming I've gone through with this work, I've grown into a far better writer than ever before.

I would easily say that the little universe I made for Maneater — or for the Dead Men's Tales franchise in general — feels like an accomplishment for me.

Christopher Linchpin

One of the main focuses of the story. Christopher Linchpin is a baker who goes on a journey through the deepest parts of Grand Guinevere to find and recover the only family member he has: his sister, Nicole.

His determination is what moves the story forward. Those who are familiar with the underworld of crime in Grand Guinevere may not know the name; but they soon will.

Nicole Linchpin

Christopher's younger sister. Nicole goes through a series of events that puts her in the hands of a gang of marginalized criminals with little regard to ethics.

Nicole's quick thinking is what brings all of the characters together to a standoff: one event that decides the rules of an entire district.

Will Nicole's worldview bend to favor her survival?

Clyde

The unfortunate underdog that followed enough orders to earn a spot. Clyde's life is harder than that of any other criminal. A well-respected man among the ranks of the Steamrollers, Clyde is a figure that stands out if compared to his peers; His ability to keep up an act for however long it requires him, alongside his loyalty and fear of the time passing is what makes him be who he is.

The sins of the father...shall not be passed down.

Solomon

Solomon is a force that exists to rival that of orderly conduct. Born amidst the slums of Grand Guinevere, the titan, leader of the Ferocious Steamrollers, is the perfect product of what Grand Guinevere has to offer. Characterized by his absurdly tall stature, rectangular face and combed thin gray hair, he is a figure many fear in Grand Guinevere, within reason.

If you play by the rules, you too become a ruler.

Grand Guinevere

Established in 1809, Grand Guinevere — formerly just Guinevere — is a sovereign state. It stands as one of the most technologically advanced powers in the known world, widely regarded, at least from the outside, as the pinnacle of modern industrial civilization.

The city’s economy is built almost entirely on technological dominance. Grand Guinevere exports machinery, mechanisms, and industrial innovations to the rest of the world, while importing skilled workmanship to sustain its ever-growing production needs. Outside scholars are rarely warmly welcomed; labor and utility matter far more than theory in the eyes of the average capitalist. The city is, after all, the birthplace of the steam engine; a fact its rulers and entrepreneurs never let the world forget.

Despite its immense wealth, Grand Guinevere is anything but equal. Most of the prosperity generated within its walls flows directly into the hands of the depths of the black market, celebrated inventors, and above all, powerful entrepreneurs. To keep revolutions at bay, many entrepreneurs keep part of their funds and flow of cash directed at the production of plays and music they approve of, redirecting the attention of the peoples to something other than their reality.

This imbalance fuels constant unrest. Famine, desperation, and unchecked inequality make violence a daily reality. Many people may turn to black markets, gangs, and other criminal enterprises as their only means of survival or social advancement. In Grand Guinevere, morality is a luxury few can afford. A proper education system as understood by you and me is mostly unheard of, being heavily influenced by the state of to-date technological advancements.

The Black Market

When the overworld beats you to a pulp and the economy isn't made for you.

The Black Market is, by definition, an illegal market in the underground sewers of Grand Guinevere. Its size is ever-growing, and the business is always blooming. It works as an alternative for those who cannot afford the luxury of being an outstanding Guineverian citizen. Many things can be found in the Black Market, ranging from stolen jewelry, to everyday tools, to stolen implants; and in the deepest depths of the Black Market, with the right contacts, even people are on the table.

Ferocious Steamrollers

The Ferocious Steamrollers are feared among the community for their recklessness. They were formed after a group split from another gang called the Rabid Dogs; their objectives did not fit into the Dogs’s culture. The Steamrollers are outright criminals, murderers and thieves who take what they want however they want on their own terms. Among their ranks, only few have any sense of empathy.

The Steamrollers are one of the most recent factions to be formed within the Guineverian ecossystem. Since a lot of the members of the gang come from other factions, it isn't unusual to see internal conflicts arising, but they operate on the basic rule that the strongest is always correct; Fights to the death aren't out of the question for many Steamrollers, as a means of protecting both honor, titles and personal goods.

Their motto is “The Steamroller is out to get ya'”.

The New Age Boys

The New Age Boys are a gang that got rejected by the system and united forces to overcome the oppression. They work like a mafia, keeping their vicinity clean and protected, but expecting to be met with ingratitude and reprimand from society.

Among other factions within Grand Guinevere, The New Age Boys are the oldest to date, having been formed between 60 to 70 years ago. Curiously, many members and branches of the New Age Boys have been reported to work in favor for the populace, sometimes taking jobs as Private Investigators, bodyguards or persecutors in cases where the Guineverian police force wouldn't get their hands on.

Their reputation precedes their presence, even in districts where their influence doesn't reach.
